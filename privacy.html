SCRAN

Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

SCRAN is a map of London restaurants that YouTube food creators have reviewed, where you can rate places and follow other people. It's a personal project run by Matt Webster. This page explains what SCRAN stores about you and who can see it.

Using SCRAN without an account

You can browse the map without signing in. Any ratings, notes or saved places you add are kept only in your browser, on your device, and never reach us.

What we store when you sign in with Google

  • From your Google account: your name, profile photo and email address. We don't get your Google password, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you add: your ratings and notes, the places you save to visit, and the people and creators you follow.

Who can see it

  • Public: your name, profile photo, ratings and notes, who you follow and who follows you. Anyone using SCRAN can see these, so don't put anything in a note you wouldn't want others to read.
  • Private: your email address and your saved places. Only you can see them.

We don't sell your information, show you ads or share it with anyone, apart from the services below that SCRAN runs on.

Services SCRAN uses

  • Supabase stores accounts and the data above.
  • Google handles signing in. Google Maps also draws the map and shows place details such as opening hours. Your browser contacts Google directly for these.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like any web host, briefly keeps standard visit logs such as your IP address.
  • Map images can also come from Esri and OpenStreetMap, and fonts from Google Fonts.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

On your device

SCRAN saves your ratings, saved places and a few settings in your browser's local storage so the map loads quickly. Signing in also stores a sign-in token there. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Open your profile (the person icon) and tap Delete my account. This removes your profile, ratings, notes, saved places and follows straight away. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you. Otherwise we keep your account for as long as you have it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within a month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

SCRAN isn't meant for children under 13, and they shouldn't sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
